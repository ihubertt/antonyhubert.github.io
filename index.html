Executive summary

M.Sc. mechanical engineer specialising in laser powder bed fusion (PBF-LB/M) process development for soft magnetic and biomedical alloys, with results published peer-reviewed in Procedia CIRP (2024). I developed an in-process laser remelting method for FeSi6.5 — a magnetically excellent but famously brittle alloy that cannot be rolled or stamped — that reduced areal surface roughness by ~60% while holding porosity below 0.2%, and that converts columnar grains to a fully equiaxed structure without embrittling the material. I bring the complete development loop to a team: parameter design and DoE, machine and component design in CAD with CFD validation, hands-on PBF-LB/M operation, and my own metallography, profilometry and SEM verification.
