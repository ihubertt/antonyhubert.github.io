About me

Metal additive manufacturing process engineer, at my most useful where a process must be developed, qualified and proven, not simply run.

Highly motivated, creative and self-directed across the whole process: judging whether a design can be made, simulating how it will behave before material is committed, preparing and running the build, then post-processing, metallography, surface measurement and the statistics that say whether a parameter set can be trusted. No stage handed away.

Structured and solution-oriented. Analytical and calm in front of a failure rather than discouraged by it, and careful to call a result true only once it has been reproduced.

Communicative across disciplines, and at ease explaining a decision to people outside the lab.

Curious, optimistic and still learning. The next step is carrying qualified processes into repeatable production.

Featured projects

Three projects, each taken from problem statement through method to quantified result.

In-process laser remelting for FeSi6.5 soft magnetic components

A heat-conduction remelting strategy that smooths the layer surface of a notoriously brittle alloy, for thin insulating layers in laminated soft magnets.

Sa 1.77 µm · published 2024 →

Inlet gas circulation system for a PBF-LB/M research machine

Designed, simulated, manufactured and integrated: active spatter evacuation, built in metal so it would tolerate the heated platform planned for the machine.

CAD · CFD · built · validated →

Porosity minimisation in laser-sintered cobalt-chromium for medical implants

Nine runs instead of twenty-seven. Mapped a tenfold porosity spread and identified which parameter actually governs it.

2.8% vs 27.06% · scan speed dominant →
